About Me

I'm passionate about bridging the gap between cutting-edge research and practical deployment. My research focuses on representation learning and foundation models for scientific and biomedical AI. Alongside academic research, I have built production machine learning systems in industry, shaping my interest in methods that are not only novel but also scalable, reproducible, and clinically meaningful.

With hands-on experience as an ML Engineer at Causalmind, I've built production systems using causal discovery and LLMs, deploying models on the cloud with Docker and managing complex data pipelines. This industry experience informs my research approach: I prioritize not just theoretical advances, but solutions that are interpretable, scalable, and clinically applicable. I believe the future of AI lies in creating foundation models that generalize across domains while remaining robust enough for healthcare and scientific applications.
